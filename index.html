Bank Reconciliation

Match a bank statement against your cash book on date and amount. Every difference is explained by type, with the next step to clear it, and the proof ties the difference to nil. The matching runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Cash book

Debit = receipt, credit = payment

Copy the lines straight from Excel and paste them in, headers included, or open a CSV or Excel file. Columns are found by name: Date, Reference, Narration (or Description), and either Amount or Debit and Credit. A single Amount column should be positive for money in and negative for money out on both sides.
