About

This tool reconciles a supplier's statement to the creditors ledger. It matches invoices, credit notes and payments, then lists what explains the difference: missing or duplicated invoices, wrong invoice numbers, keying errors, invoices captured in parts, and payments in transit.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or upload leaves your device.

Built by Chris Mukonowatsauka · linkedin.com/in/cmkn

How to use it

  1. Copy the supplier statement from Excel, a CSV or a PDF and paste it into the first box. You can also drag an Excel or CSV file onto the box, or use Upload file.
  2. Do the same with the supplier's account from your creditors ledger.
  3. Enter each opening balance, if the extracts don't start at zero.
  4. Select Reconcile. The Excel working file downloads by itself; Export to Excel downloads it again.
  5. Clear empties both sides to start the next supplier.

Columns

Date, reference, details, and amount (or debit and credit) are found automatically. If one is read wrongly, open Columns under the box and change it. Your choice is remembered next time.

The reconciliation statement

It starts from the balance per the supplier statement, lists every item that explains the difference under its heading, and ends at the balance per the ledger. The unexplained difference should be zero.

What it picks up

  • Invoices and credit notes missing from either side
  • Duplicate postings
  • Wrong or transposed invoice numbers and amounts
  • Invoices captured in parts, and postings that were reversed
  • Invoice numbers written in the description instead of the reference
  • Payments in transit and payments missing from either side

The Excel export

Three sheets: the reconciliation statement, every ledger line and every statement line, each with its status, what it was matched to, and any difference. Items flagged "Check this match" share an amount but no invoice number. Confirm them in the file.
