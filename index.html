How to use

  1. Paste or open the supplier statement on the left and your creditors ledger on the right. Click a grid and press Ctrl+V. A header row is optional, and extra columns are fine.
  2. Check the column picks above each grid. Change any with its dropdown and the app remembers your choice for files laid out the same way.
  3. Enter both opening balances so the statement and ledger balances are complete.
  4. Deal with "Check these" first: confirm or reject each suggested pair. Then work through the lines under "To look at".
  5. Export to Excel for the reconciliation statement, the ledger and the statement, each line marked Matched or Unmatched.

How lines are matched

By invoice or credit note number, wherever the clerk put it: the reference column, or inside the description when the reference holds a GRN or PO number. Parts that add up to one invoice, and postings with their reversal, clear each other. Payments match on amount within a week. Same amount with nothing else linking two lines goes to "Check these".

Settings

Payments made within the set number of days of the statement date count as in transit. Suggestions look for same-amount invoices up to the number of days you set.
